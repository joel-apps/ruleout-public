WHAT THE APP IS

Rule Out measures a room with your iPhone's sensors and checks unusual readings against ordinary causes. It is for curiosity and personal investigation. It does not detect, prove or communicate with ghosts or spirits, and an "Unexplained" verdict means only that none of the ordinary causes it can check applied.
