What's left is yours.

Rule Out turns your iPhone into an honest investigation kit. It measures a room with the phone's real sensors (magnetic field, sound and vibration), learns what's normal there, and flags anything that stands out.

Then it does what other ghost apps don't: it checks every odd moment against ordinary causes. The phone moving, electrical hum, a fridge cycling, footsteps, a door, a pet, speech from a TV. Each moment ends EXPLAINED or Unexplained, with the evidence kept: the sound, the readings, a shareable card and video.

No spirit boxes, no random word generators, no fake radars. Every number is a real reading, with its unit.

THREE WAYS TO USE IT

Something just happened: start listening in one tap.
Investigate a place: a planned session, room by room.
Overnight log: leave it running and read the morning report.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Everything stays on your phone. No account. Only the sound of flagged moments is kept, and the rest is deleted after review.
